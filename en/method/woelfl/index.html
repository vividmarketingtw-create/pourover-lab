Step by step — Recipe for 15g of coffee (15g / 239g)

Bloom + three main pours
  1. 1. Pour 1 (bloom)
    At 0:00 · Pour 53g · up to 53g · 40s
    93°C — pour 53g (22% of the total, about 3.5x the dose, more than most recipes) and swirl gently to wet all the grounds. Wait until 0:40 for pour 2 — the four pours start at a fixed 0:00 / 0:40 / 1:20 / 2:00 (the original values; they do not move with dose or your flow setting).
    Grind reference: Feima 600N setting 3.5-4 | Comandante ~21-24 clicks / 1Zpresso JX ~2.1-2.4 turns
    📏 Two published grind figures: Wildkaffee, his own roastery, states 630µm; European Coffee Trip records 490µm (Comandante C40 ~21-25 clicks). Both are public; this site does not pick a winner — start between them and adjust by total time.
    ℹ️ Competition original: 17g / 270g (1:15.9) at 93°C on an Orea V4 (Narrow + Fast base) with Sibarist FAST paper, plus a Melodrip and a needle distributor, ground fresh on stage. Four pours of 60 / 60 / 50 / 100g starting 0:00 / 0:40 / 1:20 / 2:00, last pour done by 2:20, fully drained 2:20-2:25.
    ⏱ The times on screen use a Hario V60 baseline (as they do for every method here). On a real Orea V4 Fast + Sibarist FAST you finish 20-30s sooner, closer to the original; on a V60 with standard paper the on-screen numbers are what you should see.
  2. 2. Pour 2
    At 0:40 · Pour 53g · up to 106g · 40s
    93°C, about 11s to pour 53g in small steady circles at the centre (about 4.8 g/s). Then wait until 1:20 for the next pour
  3. 3. Pour 3
    At 1:20 · Pour 44g · up to 150g · 40s
    93°C, about 9s to pour 44g, same technique. Then wait until 2:00 for the last pour
  4. 4. Pour 4 (the big one)
    At 2:00 · Pour 89g · up to 239g · 18s
    The biggest pour: 89g (about 18s, 37.0% of the total). Raise the water level to stir the bed — this is the only really assertive agitation in the whole method. Once it is in, do not touch it again.
  5. 5. Wait for drawdown
    At After pours · holding at 239g · 25s
    Fast paper drains quickly, so this finishes soon after the last pour — the on-screen target is about 2:43 (V60 baseline).
    💡 The original, on an Orea V4 Fast with Sibarist FAST paper, is fully drained at 2:20-2:25 with almost no waiting. If your total runs much longer than the screen, grind half a step coarser; much shorter and sour, half a step finer. The champion grinds fresh on the spot.

Full method guide

Origin

Austrian barista Martin Wolfl's winning recipe from the 2024 World Brewers Cup. The competition original: 17g / 270g (1:15.9) at 93°C on an Orea V4 (Narrow + Fast base) with Sibarist FAST paper, plus a Melodrip and a needle distributor, ground fresh on stage. Four pours of 60 / 60 / 50 / 100g starting 0:00 / 0:40 / 1:20 / 2:00, fully drained by 2:20-2:25.

Core idea

The core is high flow, short time: a fast cone plus fast paper keep the total under two and a half minutes so extraction stops in its cleanest, brightest window. Four escalating pours (22% bloom → 22% → 18.5% → 37.0% final) complete an even extraction quickly, with the last pour raising the level to stir the bed — the only really assertive agitation in the method. The interval is a fixed 40 seconds.

Key points

  • Pours start at a fixed 0:00 / 0:40 / 1:20 / 2:00 (the original values, locked here — they do not move with dose or flow setting)
  • Bloom with ~3.5x the dose (22% of total water) — more than most recipes
  • Pour sizes 60 / 60 / 50 / 100g, with the last one largest (37.0%) to raise the level and stir the bed
  • 93°C throughout; on the original gear the total is 2:20-2:25
  • Key gear: Orea V4 Fast bottom + Sibarist FAST paper (April or similar fast cones also work), plus a Melodrip and a needle distributor
  • Two published grind figures: Wildkaffee (his own roastery) says 630µm; European Coffee Trip says 490µm (Comandante C40 ~21-25 clicks). Start between them and adjust by total time
  • ⏱ Times shown here always use a Hario V60 baseline; the original fast cone and paper run 20-30s shorter

Beans it suits

High-quality light roasts with floral/fruity character — a short brew amplifies clean acidity and aroma

Less suited to

Medium-dark roasts or when you want heavy body (a short brew reads thin)

FAQ

No fast dripper or fast paper
A V60 with standard paper is fine — the on-screen times already assume a V60, so just follow them. To get closer to the original 2:20, grind half a step coarser
Too sour or sharp
Grind half a step finer — with a short brew, sourness usually means under-extraction. Also check the final pour really did raise the level and stir the bed
Total time much longer than the screen
Grind is too fine or there are too many fines — go half a step coarser. If it is always long, move 'My flow' toward slow
Tastes light
This style is inherently light; tighten the ratio from 1:15.9 to 1:15 if you want more presence
630µm vs 490µm — which should I trust
Both are published: Wildkaffee is his own company, ECT is press coverage. Start in the middle (about Feima 3.5-4) and dial with total time rather than chasing a micron number
